# LinkedIn Article and Poll Safety

Article packages require an explicitly approved public HTTPS URL and frozen
title and description. Confirmation does not fetch or scrape the destination,
and local, private, credential-bearing, or reserved-network URLs are rejected.

Poll packages require a frozen question, 2–4 distinct non-empty options, and an
allowlisted duration. Polls are not generated from briefings automatically and
cannot be edited through this workflow after publication.
